Expected CSV columns 
Timecard CSV columns (case-insensitive, flexible names):
  employee | name        — driver name or ID
  date                   — YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY
  scheduled_start | sched_in   — HH:MM (optional but recommended)
  scheduled_end   | sched_out
  clock_in   | actual_in | punch_in
  clock_out  | actual_out| punch_out
  edited     — Y/N or true/false (optional)
  edit_reason — free text (optional)

Itinerary CSV columns:
  employee | name
  date
  route_start | depart      — when driver left station
  first_stop  | first_delivery
  last_stop   | last_delivery
  rts | return | route_end  — return-to-station time
